tips

reading

  • hold a word → meaning, translation, and a highlight colour. tap “whole sentence” to grow the selection.
  • select text instead (in Display) turns on normal text selection with a small toolbar, for copying or highlighting any passage.
  • listen (headphones in the bottom bar) reads aloud and follows along sentence by sentence.
  • presets in Display: cosy, focus, night owl.

shelf

  • hold a book → open, details, tags, remove.
  • use search and tag chips to filter. tags are collections: “sci-fi”, “to read”, whatever.
  • find free books searches Project Gutenberg and the Internet Archive and adds straight to your shelf.

moving between devices

  • back up shelf saves one file with every book, your positions, highlights and stats. put it in iCloud Drive or Google Drive, then restore a backup on the other device. positions merge: the furthest read wins.

send to Dogear

  • Android: install Dogear from Chrome’s menu; it then appears in every app’s Share sheet.
  • iPhone: Apple doesn’t let web apps into the Share sheet. the quick route: in Mail or Safari tap the file → Share → Save to Files, then open Dogear and tap add books. a Shortcut named “Send to Dogear” that does the Save-to-Files step with one tap: open Shortcuts → + → add “Save File” (ask where: off, folder: Dogear) → add “Open URL” with this page’s address → in the Shortcut details enable “Show in Share Sheet”.
